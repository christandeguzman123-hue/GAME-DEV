Space Defender

How to play

Shoot down the invaders before they reach the bottom. Reach 1500 points to win.

Move
Left / Right arrows or A / D
Shoot
Space (hold to keep firing)
Pause
P
Start / Restart
Enter

Rules

  • You have 3 lives.
  • You lose a life when an invader hits your ship or escapes past the bottom.
  • Red invaders are worth 10 points. Purple zig-zag invaders are worth 25.
  • Every 200 points raises the level and makes invaders faster.
